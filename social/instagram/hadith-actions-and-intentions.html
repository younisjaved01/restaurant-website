Prophetic Guidance
إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ
“Indeed, actions are judged by intentions, and every person will have only what they intended.”

Every action begins with an intention.

A sincere intention can transform ordinary moments into acts of worship.

Purify your heart before your actions.

Sahih al-Bukhari 1 • Sahih Muslim 1907
